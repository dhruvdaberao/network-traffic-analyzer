Use live capture on a local machine with packet capture support, or switch to demo mode when you need reliable hosted behavior.

Demo mode

Generates realistic traffic scenarios and exercises parsing, persistence, alerts, charts, and exports without requiring packet-sniffing privileges.

Live capture requirements

Needs a supported interface, sufficient permissions, Scapy, and on Windows typically Npcap. Hosted platforms usually cannot capture your local workstation traffic.
